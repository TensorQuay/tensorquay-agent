# INC-024: ledger degraded

**Team:** storage  
**Duration:** 48 minutes  
**Severity:** SEV-2

## Summary
ledger returned errors for 48 minutes; customers saw failed requests.

## Timeline
- 00:00 alert fired
- 00:06 on-call engaged
- 00:48 resolved

## What happened
Investigation showed that ledger leaked connections' buffers and ran out of memory every few hours.

## Contributing factors
- dashboards for the service had no alert on error rate

## Follow-ups

- update the runbook
