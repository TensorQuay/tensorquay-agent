Output in a four-day week: a trial in 61 firms
Haug et al., 2023

Abstract
Output per employee stayed the same in 52 of 61 firms. Sick days fell by 65 %.

Method
We describe the setting, the data and the limits of the study.

Results
Output per employee stayed the same in 52 of 61 firms.
Sick days fell by 65 %.
