# Architecture decision records

Every ADR identifier comes from the decision index in
[ARCHITECTURE section 18](../ARCHITECTURE.md#18-decision-records-to-write-docsadr).
Reserve a decision there before adding its record; never reuse or renumber an identifier.
Accepted records are retained, and a later record may supersede them.

Use this template for each record:

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: Title

## Status

Proposed, Accepted or Superseded (link the replacement).

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The problem, constraints and alternatives that require a decision.

## Decision

The chosen approach and the reason for it.

## Consequences

The benefits, costs and obligations introduced by the decision.

## Links

Related requirements, architecture sections and decision records.
```
