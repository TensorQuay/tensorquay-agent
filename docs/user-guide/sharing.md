# Sharing

<!-- Tests: WEB-U-023, WEB-U-025. -->
You share workspaces, not tasks. Your tasks, with their messages and the agent's work, stay yours.

## Share a workspace
On the **Workspaces** page, choose **Share** on a workspace you own. Enter the email address of a person with an
account on this installation and choose their **Access**:

| Access | They can |
|---|---|
| **Can view** | Open its files and copy it |
| **Can edit** | Open its files and start their own tasks on it |

The **Members** list shows who has access; change a person's access there, or choose **Remove** to take it away. A
shared workspace shows **Shared** with the number of people on its card.

People you share with never see your tasks, and you never see theirs. They see the files, which include anything your
tasks wrote there. Anyone with **Can edit** can change the files through their own tasks, so make a copy of the
workspace first if you want to keep a version nobody else changes.

## What administrators can see
An administrator can see that a task exists and its details (owner, dates, environment, model and state), but not its
content. Opening another person's task shows **Private task** with those details only.

## Inviting people
Only an administrator can create accounts. They send an invitation link; opening it shows **Join** with the role you
are invited as. If the page says the link is not valid, it may have expired or been used already: ask your
administrator for a new one.
